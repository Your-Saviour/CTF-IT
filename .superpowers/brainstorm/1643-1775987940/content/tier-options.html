What should the tiers be?

All VMs appear flat within their tier — no sub-grouping or role separation

Event → all VMs flat

Two tiers. Event on top, all VMs in one row below regardless of role. Simplest possible structure.

WAN → Event → all VMs flat

Three tiers. A generic WAN/internet node at top, then event, then all VMs flat. More closely mirrors a real network diagram with an upstream connection.

Attacker VMs get their own tier below targets, with dashed "attack path" lines going up. Visually emphasizes the red team / blue team dynamic.
